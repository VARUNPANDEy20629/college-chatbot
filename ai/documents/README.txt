Put the college's official PDF or text documents in this folder. The chatbot's generic guidance should be replaced or supplemented with these documents before it is published to students.

Recommended knowledge base:
- College_Profile_and_Contacts.pdf: college name, address, phone numbers, email addresses, office hours, departments, and emergency contacts
- Admission_Prospectus.pdf: programmes, eligibility, documents, application steps, deadlines, and seat information
- Fee_Structure_and_Refunds.pdf: tuition, other charges, payment methods, deadlines, instalments, refunds, and late fees
- Academic_Regulations.pdf: attendance, credits, grading, leave, promotion, conduct, and academic calendar
- Exam_Regulations_and_Schedule.pdf: registration, timetable, hall ticket, rules, revaluation, supplementary exams, and results
- Course_Syllabi_and_Timetables.pdf: departments, subjects, credits, rooms, faculty, and class schedules
- Library_Rules_and_Schedule.pdf: hours, borrowing, renewals, fines, databases, and holidays
- Hostel_Handbook.pdf: application, allocation, fees, mess, visitors, leave, safety, and maintenance
- Transport_Routes_and_Rules.pdf: routes, stops, timings, passes, fees, parking, and safety
- Scholarship_and_Financial_Aid.pdf: eligibility, documents, deadlines, renewal, and contacts
- Student_Services_and_Forms.pdf: ID cards, bonafide, transcripts, transfer, migration, certificates, and forms
- Placement_and_Internship_Guide.pdf: eligibility, companies, registration, internships, resume rules, and interview process
- Student_Welfare_and_Grievance_Policy.pdf: counselling, health, anti-ragging, harassment, complaints, and escalation contacts
- IT_Services_Guide.pdf: portal, email, LMS, Wi-Fi, password reset, and helpdesk procedure

Keep dates, fees, rules, contacts, and emergency information current. Do not add private student records or passwords.
